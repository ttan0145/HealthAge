Stay Fit

A simple starter routine

This routine is not medical advice. It is a light activity prompt for generally healthy adults. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

1Brisk walk10 minutes
2Chair squats2 sets of 8
3Wall push-ups2 sets of 8
4Standing calf raises2 sets of 10

Guidance

Start gently and keep the effort conversational. If you have not exercised recently or have an existing condition, check with a health professional first.
