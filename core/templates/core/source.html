Data transparency

Sources used by HealthAge

HealthAge uses public Malaysian health statistics to prioritise one practical next step. The result is informational and should not be read as a medical diagnosis.

Mortality

DOSM Statistics on Causes of Death

Department of Statistics Malaysia, 2021 to 2024

Named causes of death by age band, state and sex, used to identify the leading risk for a profile.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

Risk factors

NHMS 2023/2024

Institute for Public Health

National Health and Morbidity Survey risk factor rates, such as smoking and inactivity, used as population context.

Licence: public report, Institute for Public Health

Deaths

OpenDOSM Annual Deaths

Department of Statistics Malaysia

Annual death count publications used to support demographic mortality context.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

Life expectancy

OpenDOSM Life Expectancy Tables

Department of Statistics Malaysia

Life expectancy and population estimates used to explain mortality trends relative to demographic groups.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

Screening

KKMNOW Daily PeKaB40 Screenings

Ministry of Health Malaysia

Daily PeKaB40 screening records used to explain why screening may be the first practical action for eligible users.

Licence: open data, Ministry of Health Malaysia

Exercise

wger.de Exercise Database

wger.de

Open exercise catalogue used to support future personalised exercise recommendations.

Licence: open source, AGPL-3.0

Specialist

Specialist Search

Ministry of Health Malaysia

Hospital specialist directory reserved for future redirect actions based on an identified health concern.

Licence: public directory, Ministry of Health Malaysia
