DOSM Statistics on Causes of Death

Department of Statistics Malaysia, 2021 to 2024

Named causes of death by age band, state and sex, used to identify your leading risk. Published at dosm.gov.my.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

NHMS 2023/2024

Institute for Public Health, NHMS 2023

National Health and Morbidity Survey risk factor rates, such as smoking and inactivity, used to compare your habits with the national baseline.

Licence: public report, Institute for Public Health

OpenDOSM Annual Deaths (District / State / Sex / Ethnicity)

Department of Statistics Malaysia

Department of Statistics Malaysia, annual dataset. Demographic death counts by district, state, sex and ethnicity, used to match users with the most relevant mortality statistics.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

OpenDOSM Life Expectancy & Population Tables

Department of Statistics Malaysia

Department of Statistics Malaysia, annual life expectancy and population estimates. Provides life expectancy and population context to help users understand mortality trends relative to their demographic group.

Licence: open data, Department of Statistics Malaysia

KKMNOW Daily PeKaB40 Screenings

Ministry of Health Malaysia

Ministry of Health Malaysia (KKMNOW). Daily PeKaB40 health screening locations and schedules, used to recommend nearby screening opportunities for eligible users.

Licence: open data, Ministry of Health Malaysia

wger.de Exercise Database

wger.de

wger Project, open exercise database. Exercise library with muscle groups, instructions and equipment information, used to recommend personalised physical activities based on users' health goals.

Licence: open source (AGPL-3.0), wger Project

Ministry of Health Malaysia. Hospital specialist directory used to help users locate relevant medical specialists based on their identified health concern.
