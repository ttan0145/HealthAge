This is not a diagnosis

Health Age does not diagnose you and does not predict what will happen to you. It compares the profile you enter against national mortality statistics to show which risks are worth prioritising. Only a health professional can assess your own health.
Data: DOSM Statistics on Causes of Death 2021 to 2024, NHMS 2023, KKMNOW PeKaB40 screenings, wger.de. Built by Team TM16, FIT5120 Industry Experience, Monash University Malaysia.
